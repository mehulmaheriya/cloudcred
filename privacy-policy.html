Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026. Your privacy and corporate anonymity are strictly guaranteed across every transaction.

1. Overview & Commitment to Anonymity

CloudCred ("CloudCred.store", "we", "us", or "our") operates an anonymous secondary marketplace for cloud compute and AI API credits. We recognize that selling unallocated startup credits or purchasing secondary cloud capacity requires total confidentiality and data protection.

Our foundational commitment is **Zero Public Exposure**. Neither your identity, company legal name, email address, nor corporate affiliation is ever disclosed on public marketplace listings or shared with third-party cloud providers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to facilitate escrow verification and execute secure deals:

  • Inquiry & Contact Details: Email address, Telegram handle, or WhatsApp number supplied voluntarily via our contact or submission forms.
  • Credit Allocation Specifications: Provider name (e.g. OpenAI, AWS, GCP, Anthropic), estimated balance value, expiration dates, and account transfer method (Full account, Org invite, API key).
  • Settlement Details: Wallet addresses (USDT / USDC / BTC) or bank wire routing info provided during active escrow settlement.

3. How Information is Used & Mutual NDA

Information provided to CloudCred is strictly utilized for:

  • Matching sellers with pre-screened buyers under a legally binding Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
  • Verifying credit balances and account authorization in isolated, secure deal rooms.
  • Dispatched escrow payouts upon successful credential handoff.

We **never sell, rent, or trade** personal data or inquiry details to marketers, data brokers, or cloud providers.

4. Escrow & Security Protocols

All sensitive transactions occur through encrypted channels. Credentials exchanged during account handovers are held in transient, end-to-end encrypted storage and purged automatically within 72 hours of escrow completion.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You retain the right to request full erasure of your contact history and submitted credit inquiries at any time. Simply contact our privacy desk via Telegram or WhatsApp, and all associated transient records will be immediately purged.
